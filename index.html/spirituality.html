☸️ Buddha

Understand Your Mind

The Buddha's teachings focus on understanding suffering, desire, thoughts, and the mind.

  • Everything changes — don't become too attached.
  • Your thoughts influence your actions.
  • Anger usually hurts the person carrying it.
  • Desire can create dissatisfaction.
  • Observe your mind instead of blindly following every thought.
  • Be kind, but don't lose wisdom.
  • Your actions have consequences.
  • Peace comes from understanding yourself, not controlling everything around you.
Simple practice: Stop → Observe → Understand → Respond.
🧘

Vipassana — Observe Reality

Vipassana means seeing things as they are. The practice traditionally involves observing the breath and bodily sensations without reacting to them.

  • Pleasant experience → observe it.
  • Unpleasant experience → observe it.
  • Thoughts arise → observe them.

The lesson: Everything arises and passes away.

Start simple: sit quietly for 5–10 minutes, observe your natural breathing, and return your attention whenever the mind wanders.

🕯️

A Simple Practice

Sit quietly. Notice your breathing. Observe thoughts as they arise. Don't fight them and don't blindly follow them. Return your attention to the present moment.
